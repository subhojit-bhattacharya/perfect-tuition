We collect: your name, your phone number, the class and subjects you're asking about, and anything you write in the message box.

We use it for one purpose only: to contact you about tuition for your child at Perfect Tuition. We use it for no other purpose, and we do not share your details with anyone else.

Please do not include your child's name, school, or address.

We keep this information for 24 months, then delete it. The law requires us to keep records for at least one year, so if you ask us to delete yours sooner, we will delete it as soon as that year is up.

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your information at any time, or withdraw your consent, by contacting Jayeeta Bhattacharya at privacy@perfect-tuition.co.in. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
